Privacy Policy

What this site does not do

No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising or tracking scripts, no database, and no third-party scripts or fonts.


What it stores on your device

One browser flag recording your 18+ confirmation, plus cached copies of the pages so the site works offline. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes both.


Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. I do not receive or use those logs.


Contact

The only contact channel is SimpleX, which uses no phone number or username. Messages auto-delete. I never ask for your real name, address or photos, and I never sell, share or publish anything you tell me.


Deleting your data

Message me on the official SimpleX address and ask me to delete our chat. Full steps are on the report page.


Adults only

This site is for adults 18+ only. Anyone under 18 must leave immediately.
