Consent and boundaries: a clear guide

Consent is what makes any adult connection safe and respectful. It is simple once you know what to look for.

What consent is

  • Freely given. No pressure, guilt, flattery used as a weapon, or fear.
  • Clear and enthusiastic. A yes you mean, not a silence or a shrug.
  • Specific. Agreeing to one thing is not agreeing to everything.
  • Reversible. You can change your mind at any moment, even after saying yes.
  • Informed and sober. Someone who is intoxicated or asleep cannot consent.

How to set your boundaries

Decide before you meet what you are comfortable with and what is off the table. Say it plainly in the chat first. A respectful person will thank you for being clear and will not argue with it.

How to say no

You can say no kindly or firmly, and you never need a reason. Simple lines work: "I'm not comfortable with that" or "I've changed my mind". If the other person keeps pushing, that is your answer about them.

What respect looks like

  • They ask, and they listen to the answer.
  • They check in and accept a pause or a stop without sulking.
  • They never make you feel guilty for protecting yourself.

If a boundary is crossed

It is never your fault. Leave, go to a safe place, and tell someone you trust. In an emergency contact local emergency services.
