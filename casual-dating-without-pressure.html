Casual dating without pressure

Some adults want connection without commitment. That is a valid choice when everyone involved is honest about it and treated with respect.

What "no strings" really means

It means both people understand there is no promise of a relationship. It does not mean no manners, no honesty or no care. Being casual is no excuse for disrespect.

Talk about expectations early

  • Say what you want and what you do not want, plainly.
  • Ask what the other person is looking for, and believe the answer.
  • Agree on how you will communicate, and what happens afterward.

Keep your privacy

You decide what to share. A first name or nickname is enough. Keep your address, workplace and finances to yourself. Discretion should work both ways.

Signs it is going well

  • You feel calm, not rushed.
  • Their words and actions match.
  • They respect your answers, including "no" and "not yet".

Signs to walk away

  • Pressure to move faster than you want.
  • Requests for money, gifts or favours.
  • Refusing a video call or avoiding simple questions.
