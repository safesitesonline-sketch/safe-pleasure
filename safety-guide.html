Safety guide for meeting someone new

Meeting someone you only know online deserves care, whatever the reason. These steps are simple, free and work for women in Lagos, Ogun State and anywhere else.

Before you meet

  • Chat first. Take as long as you need. Anyone who rushes you is showing you something about themselves.
  • Do a short video call. It confirms he looks and sounds like his photos. A person who refuses is a red flag.
  • Tell a trusted friend. Share his name, his number and where you are going.
  • Keep your details private. Do not share your home address, workplace or financial information.

On the day

  • Choose a public place. A busy, well-lit restaurant or lounge in an area you know, such as in Lagos Island, the Mainland or a main town in Ogun State.
  • Arrange your own transport there and back, and keep your phone charged.
  • Share your live location with your friend for the whole meeting.
  • Watch your drink and never leave it unattended.

You can always leave

You never owe anyone an explanation. If something feels wrong, leave, go somewhere with other people and call your friend. Trust that feeling.

Protection and health

Protection is used every time, with no exceptions and no negotiation. If anyone pressures you about this, end the meeting.

Never send money

Nobody legitimate asks you for money, gifts, airtime or transfers. This is a free service, and any request for money is a scam. Stop and block the person.

Ready to say hello?

No account, no payment, no pressure. You set the pace.
